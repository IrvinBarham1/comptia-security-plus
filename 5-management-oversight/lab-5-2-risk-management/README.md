# Lab 5.2 — Security Program Management and Oversight

## 🎯 Objective
**“Explain elements of the risk management process.”**

This lab explains how organizations **identify, assess, analyze, and manage risks** to maintain business continuity and resilience.  
It covers risk assessment methods, quantitative analysis models, management strategies, and business impact metrics that support decision-making and governance reporting.

---

## 📖 Background

Risk management ensures that an organization can **anticipate threats, measure their potential impact, and implement controls** to reduce exposure.  
Through continuous assessment and reporting, security teams balance protection goals with operational and financial realities.  
Quantitative and qualitative approaches help define acceptable levels of risk based on the organization’s **tolerance and appetite**.

### Core Focus Areas

| Category | Purpose | Example Topics |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Risk Identification** | Discover assets, threats, and vulnerabilities that may affect operations | Asset inventory, Threat mapping, Vulnerability discovery |
| **Risk Assessment** | Determine when and how often risks are evaluated | Ad hoc, One-time, Recurring, Continuous |
| **Risk Analysis** | Measure likelihood and impact using data-driven or judgment-based methods | Qualitative, Quantitative, SLE, ALE, ARO, Exposure Factor |
| **Risk Register** | Track and assign accountability for identified risks | Risk indicators, Owners, Thresholds |
| **Risk Appetite & Tolerance** | Define acceptable levels of exposure for strategic decision-making | Expansionary, Conservative, Neutral |
| **Risk Management Strategies** | Implement methods to control, transfer, or accept risks | Mitigate, Avoid, Transfer, Accept |
| **Risk Reporting** | Communicate risk status and mitigation progress to leadership | Risk dashboards, Executive summaries |
| **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)** | Measure operational and recovery expectations | RTO, RPO, MTTR, MTBF |
