# Lab 5.3 — Security Program Management and Oversight

## 🎯 Objective
**“Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management.”**

This lab focuses on how organizations **evaluate, contract, and monitor vendors** to reduce risk across the supply chain.  
It demonstrates the importance of vendor due diligence, contract controls, and continuous oversight to ensure external partners meet security and compliance expectations.

---

## 📖 Background

Third-party risk management ensures that vendors and suppliers handling organizational data or services maintain **comparable security standards**.  
Assessments, contractual safeguards, and monitoring activities provide transparency and accountability throughout the vendor lifecycle.  
Without these controls, organizations face potential exposure from weak external security practices or unverified dependencies.

### Core Focus Areas

| Category | Purpose | Example Topics |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Vendor Assessment** | Evaluate a vendor’s security posture and compliance readiness | Penetration Testing, Right-to-Audit, Internal Audits, Independent Assessments, Supply Chain Analysis |
| **Vendor Selection** | Identify reliable partners and avoid conflicts | Due Diligence, Conflict of Interest |
| **Agreement Types** | Define expectations and obligations in formal contracts | SLA, MOA, MOU, MSA, SOW/WO, NDA, BPA |
| **Vendor Monitoring** | Maintain ongoing assurance of compliance and performance | Continuous Review, Risk Metrics, Compliance Audits |
| **Questionnaires** | Gather standardized data on vendor controls and policies | Security Self-Assessment, Compliance Disclosure |
| **Rules of Engagement** | Establish boundaries for authorized testing or joint operations | Pen Test Authorization, Testing Scope, Legal Approval |
