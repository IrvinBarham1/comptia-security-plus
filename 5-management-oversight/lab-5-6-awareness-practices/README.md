# Lab 5.6 — Security Program Management and Oversight

## 🎯 Objective
**“Given a scenario, implement security awareness practices.”**

This lab strengthens your understanding of how to **educate, train, and empower users** to recognize and respond to security threats.  
It focuses on phishing awareness, anomalous behavior recognition, user training, and continuous reporting—critical elements of a strong organizational security culture.

---

## 📖 Background

Human error remains one of the leading causes of security incidents.  
An effective awareness program builds a **culture of accountability and vigilance**, ensuring that every user recognizes threats such as phishing, insider activity, or social engineering.  
Regular reporting, training, and reinforcement help organizations maintain compliance and reduce risk exposure across hybrid and remote environments.

### Core Focus Areas

| Category | Purpose | Example Topics |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Phishing Awareness** | Identify, report, and respond to social engineering attempts | Simulated Phishing Campaigns, Recognizing Suspicious Emails, Reporting Malicious Messages |
| **Anomalous Behavior Recognition** | Detect irregular or high-risk activity | Risky, Unexpected, and Unintentional Behaviors |
| **User Guidance and Training** | Reinforce secure daily habits and operational awareness | Policy/Handbooks, Insider Threat, Passwords, Removable Media, Social Engineering, OPSEC, Hybrid/Remote Work |
| **Reporting and Monitoring** | Maintain accountability and visibility | Initial Incident Reporting, Recurring Awareness Reviews |
| **Program Development** | Design and plan training programs | Objective Setting, Curriculum Design, Risk Alignment |
| **Program Execution** | Deliver and evaluate awareness campaigns | Course Launch, Phishing Simulations, Engagement Metrics |
